## Technology & Safety

### Q: How do you handle suicidality or safety concerns during a session?

Safety is foundational. The AI includes real-time monitoring for suicidal ideation and harm language. Alerts are immediately flagged to your care team via your preferred protocol. We deploy in-clinic (not remote yet) specifically so staff can intervene if needed. You choose the threshold: interrupt immediately, or collect and flag without disrupting flow to avoid stigmatization. Separate AI safety models run continuously during every session.

### Q: Is patient data secure? Where is it stored?

Yes. HIPAA-compliant infrastructure with SOC 2 Type 2 in progress. AES-256 encryption, zero data retention with AI vendors (BAAs enforced), automatic de-identification. Servers are located in the US (or regionally for international deployments). We offer flexible retention: keep data for analytics, or zero retention once delivered to your EHR, it's your choice. No PHI stored on VR devices.

### Q: Why VR? Isn't that complicated?

VR drives better data: our MIT trial showed higher disclosure of sensitive information (trauma, substance use, SDOH) versus standard intake. Patients find it a safer space — they're not facing a person, which reduces judgment anxiety. Setup is under 5 minutes with consumer-grade hardware. Patients self-guide after handoff; 77% wanted to use it again.

## Implementation & Integration

### Q: How does this integrate with our EHR?

Step 1: standalone portal with one-click copy. Step 2: In-Basket PDF via Redox (200+ EHRs including Epic, Cerner/Millennium, Athena). Step 3: full SSO if desired. During a pilot, our team can support data entry to avoid taking away your team's time, no IT project required. We adapt to your workflow, not the other way around.

### Q: Our staff isn't very tech-savvy. Will this actually work?

We designed for late adopters, not early adopters. MA training is 1 hour, they're handing off a headset, not operating complex software. Patients self-guide through sessions. We provide hands-on implementation: we come to you, set everything up, train staff in person, and stay involved until everyone's comfortable. The technology adapts to different comfort levels, and we're there to support through the learning curve.

### Q: Can the reports be customized to what we need?

Yes. Reports cover up to 39 clinical domains, but you choose what matters. If you care most about social support, sleep, and coping mechanisms we focus the conversation there. If certain domains aren't relevant to your population, we remove them. The AI conversation adapts to your clinical priorities, and reports reflect only what you need.

### Q: Does it work for patients with lower reading levels or accessibility needs?

Yes. All content is designed at a 4th-5th grade reading level — essential for health literacy. The AI adapts conversationally based on patient responses. Future development includes demographic and cultural adaptation. Patients who struggle with paper forms often do better with conversational AI because they can just talk.

### Q: Who else is using this?

Active pilots in MA, NY, and CA systems. First roll out is with a psychiatry clinic. MIT IRB-approved trial with 50 patients. Dozens of specialists across renowned institutions validated our reports.

### Q: What if it doesn't work for us?

90-day pilot with ROI visible in 30 days. We provide hands-on support — we come to you, set up the technology, train your staff, and stay involved. If no value, you're out $7,500 in implementation and coordination time. We even offer free trials of specific modules so you can see how staff and patients respond before committing.
