## Section 5: The Avatar

*[LIGHT BACKGROUND — Cream #F5F0E8]*

**THE INTERFACE**

**Why a non-human avatar.**

*[TWO-COLUMN LAYOUT — video left, text right (opposite of VR section for visual variety)]*

*[LEFT COLUMN:]*

*[VIDEO: Robot Head Movements Mar 27.mp4 — autoplay, muted, loop. Rounded-2xl container, overflow-hidden. No audio. Prominently sized — at least 400px wide on desktop.]*

*[RIGHT COLUMN:]*
Research in human-computer interaction shows that people disclose more to agents they perceive as non-human. The absence of a human face removes the fear of judgment, social desirability bias, and the self-editing that shapes every clinical encounter.

AugMend's conversational avatar is deliberately non-human. It creates psychological distance from the clinical relationship while maintaining the warmth and responsiveness of a guided conversation. Patients don't perform for it. They speak to it.

This matters most for the information clinicians need and rarely get: trauma history, substance use, psychosocial stressors, and the emotional dimensions of chronic illness. In our trial data, AI-mediated responses showed significantly higher emotional intensity (p < .001) and greater emotional diversity (p = .04) — patients are not just saying more, they're saying what they actually feel.

# 5. OUR STORY

*[DARK BACKGROUND — Deep Space #0D0B3E] [BREADCRUMB] Home / Company / Our Story*

*[VISUAL: Face-with-dots portrait as background — subtle, atmospheric, not hero-scale]*

**WHY WE EXIST**

**The provider does not have enough time with the patient. When they do, they are pulled in too many directions to capture the full picture. The patient is not feeling heard. The clinic loses on both quality and revenue.**

*[White background]*

There is a gap in medicine between what a patient carries and what the clinical encounter captures. Complex patients — those managing chronic pain, behavioral health conditions, overlapping psychosocial stressors — present with information that a 15-minute visit cannot surface. Not because providers do not know what to ask. Because there is not enough time to ask it all, document it all, and still be present with the patient.

Providers need help. Patients need to be heard. Clinics need the documentation that reflects the complexity of care they deliver. No existing tool sits at this intersection.

**WHAT WE BUILT**

AugMend Health is a platform that creates the conditions for patients to share the full complexity of their health story — before, during, and between visits — so providers can focus on treatment and the patient relationship.

Self-guided AI sessions collect biopsychosocial data and social determinants of health across clinical domains. Two structured reports — clinical and billing — are generated from every session. Providers arrive informed. Documentation reflects reality. Revenue follows.

We built this at MIT, out of years of research in virtual reality and patient engagement. Our co-founder's research demonstrated that patients engage more deeply and disclose more in immersive digital environments than through standard assessment. That finding became the foundation of everything we do.
